## Steps (total about 4-5 hours spread over 3 days)

1. Read the rules of 2-3 target campaigns (30 min). Start with KreekCraft, Dylan Page, AsianJeff: per-view, no application. Pools are a gamble.
2. Create a Content Rewards clipper account (15 min). [VERIFIED] The homepage links "Click Here to Start Earning" (contentrewards.com/c/get-started/enter). Creators connect TikTok/IG/YouTube accounts, join a campaign, post, then submit. (Source 1) Do this yourself, with your own login.
3. Set up payouts (20 min). [VERIFIED] Whop processes payouts. Content Rewards balance to Whop balance is free. Whop to bank/PayPal/wallet costs a provider fee (Source 4). [UNCERTAIN] Whop docs say bank/wallet payouts in 200+ countries and list EU bank transfers in EUR (Source 5). Denmark is not named, and nothing confirms Content Rewards clippers get the same methods. Check Whop's Balances page for what Denmark shows. Payout cycle: 7 days earning plus 3 days hold, about every 10 days (Source 3). Minimum payout and ID checks: not stated.
4. Taxes (15 min, [UNCERTAIN] general knowledge). Clip income is taxable in Denmark. Check skat.dk on how to report it (likely personal income or small business). Ask Skattestyrelsen or an accountant.
5. Create the 3 posting accounts (60 min). Use one email set, 2FA on, public profiles. Campaigns say: account themed to the streamer (Lauren Hamden), GTA 6 account (RUCKUS). [UNCERTAIN] No minimum account age was found. Do not buy followers or views.
   - Handles (check availability yourself): `gta6.clipvault`, `viceclips.daily`, `leonida.moments`.
   - Bio: "Daily GTA 6 clips from the biggest streams. Follow for the funniest moments. Not affiliated with Rockstar." (TikTok, IG). YouTube: same text in the channel description.
   - Make one account per platform. Keep all three in the same niche.
6. Warm-up routine, 20-30 min per day on days 1-3 (general advice, [UNCERTAIN]; no campaign requires it):
   - Day 1: watch GTA 6 and streamer content for 20 min on each app, like 10-15, follow 10 streamers and clip accounts, no posting.
   - Day 2: same, plus comment on 5 posts. Post 1 test clip on TikTok only.
   - Day 3: post 1-2 clips per platform. Do not mass-post or follow hundreds of accounts.
7. Register a free Twitch developer app (15 min). Log in at https://dev.twitch.tv/console [VERIFIED, Source 6]. Turn on two-factor authentication on twitch.tv first (required).
   - Applications tab > Register Your Application.
   - Name: unique, e.g. `clipfinder-<yourname>`.
   - OAuth Redirect URL: `http://localhost`, click Add.
   - Category: pick the closest to "Application Integration" or "Other" [UNCERTAIN: exact list not on the doc page].
   - Complete the CAPTCHA, click Create, then Manage on your app.
   - The Client ID is on the Manage page (public). Copy it.
   - DO NOT paste the Client Secret (button "New Secret") into chat. Keep it out of any message. The Client ID is enough for most read-only lookups, and the build session can ask how to store the secret safely.
8. First test clip (45 min). Download a test VOD, cut 20-40 s, add the paid disclosure at the start, add required tags and overlay, post, submit inside 30 min.
9. Check results after 24 h (10 min). Confirm approval, view counts and the campaign budget left.
