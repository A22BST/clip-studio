## How to decide whether these ideas work

- Start with ranks 1-4 on the same permitted VODs. Review clips blind for understandable setup, visible/audible payoff, truthful hook, clean cuts and readable framing; record rejection reasons and runtime.
- For candidate changes, preserve the fixed clip budget, duration coverage and random baseline from [hype research](hype-research.md). Evaluate on fresh whole VODs, with quiet moments included; do not tune and grade against the same viewer clips.
- For performance learning, join by explicit video ID rather than fuzzy titles. Compare similar-age clips within creator/campaign cohorts; record missing data and sample counts. Retention is stronger diagnostic evidence than raw views alone, but correlations still do not prove which edit caused an outcome.
- Do not transplant the reference's thresholds or claimed speed. Its title advice also conflicts internally: selection favors creator names first, while stats diagnosis favors verbs. Test packaging that truthfully matches the clip instead of enforcing either as a universal rule.

## Must NOT do

- **License:** the supplied project has no license file. Copy no code, prompts, configuration tables, sound synthesis recipes, bundled sounds, fonts or models. Independently specify and implement behavior; obtain separately licensed assets and weights with recorded provenance.
- **Permission:** do not import `clip_rules.json`'s creator labels or treat press articles, silence, credit, or a public video as a grant. Preserve our campaign/written-permission requirement; commentary or effects do not substitute for permission.
- **ToS/access:** do not reproduce the reference's embedded third-party Twitch client identity, undocumented discovery calls, ad-skipping flags or browser-cookie workarounds. Use approved access methods, official APIs or an audited scheduler where applicable; verify platform terms before implementing live capture or stats access. No accounts, keys, logins, posting or external actions were used for this review.
- **Fake engagement:** never buy views, run engagement bots or pods, or automate likes/comments. Learning must use legitimate observed results; recommendations must not trigger automatic reposting or publishing.
- **Unsafe automation:** do not inherit live mode's keep-on-AI-error behavior as approval. Failed or skipped checks remain unresolved; a QC PASS is not a guarantee of editorial quality. Do not hardcode a camera/gameplay seam, equate white pixels with captions, reject every dark scene, or enable the reference's noisy wanted-star heuristic without validation.
- **Content:** preserve our existing three spoiler levels and campaign limits. No leaks, isolated cutscene uploads, misleading hooks, or effects that hide required attribution/disclosure. Do not copy the reference's blanket spoiler ban over our permitted reaction workflow.
